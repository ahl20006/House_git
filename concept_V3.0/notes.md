# Taif / Palm House · Concept C1 · decisions log

## Decided
- Build on the firm, higher ground in the north of the plot; the palms stay outside the house.
- Courtyard 6 × 5 m at the centre, open to the sky, enclosed on all four sides.
- North of the courtyard: only the women's majlis. Women guests enter through the family door only.
- East band (4 m): kitchen (isolated, its only door to the living), laundry, maid room, maid bath.
- South of the courtyard: double-height living room; dining sits inside it.
- West service band (4.5 m), outer side north → south: family lobby (entrance), air tower, service shaft, lift, stair, family WC. Riwaq (1.75 m) on the courtyard side, closed wall with windows onto the courtyard.
- South-west: guest entry + WC + basins; men's majlis 10 × 5.5 m with its own entrance, no door to the family side. Solid wall between the guest WC and the family WC (shared plumbing wall).
- Air: tower 2.75 × 2 m beside the service shaft, catches the NW wind above the roof; air goes down to an underground tunnel and enters the courtyard through a low, closable opening in its NW corner. The tunnel is described, not drawn.
- Files: base.typ (site, fixed) · design.typ (shift, rotation, text) · data/rooms.csv (rooms; # switches a row off).

## Services built for life (V3.0, 11 Oct 2026)
- The service shaft sits INSIDE a utility room (2.75 × 2.5 m): shaft, main electrical panel, water manifold with isolation valves, water heater (ventilated) and cleaning equipment, all behind one door from the riwaq.
- Shaft: closed box ≈ 1.0 × 1.2 m clear (≈ 1.5 × 1.2 m with walls), sealed walls, one full-height maintenance door facing east; a 0.9 m clear zone in front of that door is never used for storage.
- The shaft is in the corner touching the family basins, so drainage runs stay short; first-floor bathrooms must stack above or beside it.
- Store under the stair's half landing (≈ 2.75 × 1.0 m, ≈ 1.6 m headroom), door onto the riwaq; drawn inside the stair, not counted as extra area.
- Space saved (≈ 1.3 m) went to the family wash rooms: Family basins (2) 2.75 × 2.8 m (7.7 m²), Family WC + basin 2.75 × 3.5 m (9.6 m²). The family WC is still back-to-back with the guest WC across one solid plumbing wall.
- Code note: the Saudi Building Code sets no fixed size for a closed pipe shaft; ≈ 0.6 × 0.6 m is the practical minimum for a pipe-only duct and ≈ 1.0 × 1.2 m is comfortable for a two-storey villa. Sources were general professional guidance, not the code text; confirm with the SBC, the municipality and the plumbing engineer.
