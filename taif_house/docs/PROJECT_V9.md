# Taif / Palm House · V9.2 · master document · layout freeze, final site position

Concept design of a two-storey courtyard house in Taif. This document describes the whole house as of **V9.2** (git tag `v9.2`, 8 Nov 2026: small fixes: the utility room's label, **the air tower back to a rectangle of 0.90 m² clear** with the wedge beside it given to the maid bath and bedroom 1, the HRV base at its V9.0 footprint, **the attic 2 cm lower** so it is hidden from the north boundary) and **V9.1** (git tag `v9.1`, 8 Nov 2026): **the final site position**, corner B1 exactly **6.00 m from M1** after the owner's car test on site (a full forward turn into the north strip without any manoeuvre needs it; a further 0.51 m south), and **the cut wall shortened again** (a further 0.24 m outwards) so it keeps **0.42 m from the boundary wall as built** (0.91 m from the deed setback line); the set-out sheet re-issued, the site items updated, the levels to be set from the official survey. The layout stays frozen (§5) since **V9.0, the layout freeze** (git tag `v9.0`, 6 Nov 2026), which added to V8.3 the **site set out on site** by the owner (day 1, the diagonals exact) and the **final position** (1.24 + 0.50 m further south, corner B1 5.49 m from peg M1); the **cut wall 0.12 m shorter** after the field reading of 2.30 m at its tightest point (0.42 m from the boundary wall as built, 0.90 m from the deed setback line); the **whole-farm base v2** (the deed is the boundary authority) with **the old house and its majlis, the covered parking, the water tanks and the septic** placed from the owner's answers (approximate, to be measured); and **bedroom 1 as the young children's room without an en-suite** (§4.54–4.60). V8.0–V8.3 brought the fire safety, the courtyard layout, the air system, the south wing v2, the roof ladder, the stair flip, the site and services plan, the plumbing study and decisions, bedroom 2's en-suite and the family WC, the powder room, the level courtyard, the outdoor kitchen by the service door and the facade principle R-37 (§4.31–4.53). The open items are listed in `OPEN_ITEMS.md`, the next steps in `NEXT_SESSION.md`, the field record in `SITE_SETOUT.md`. The figures below come from the project files: `data/*.csv`, the design files and the studies. They match the presentation `output/Taif_House_V9.0_presentation.pdf` (12 sheets; sheet 01 the site, sheet 06 the south wing and the suite, sheet 09 the decisions, sheets 11–12 the roof and the elevations) and the review status `output/Design_Review_status_V9.0.pdf`. The plumbing study, the site plan and the whole-farm study are studies: their positions and sizes are proposals until the survey and the engineers confirm them.

*Concept design, not for construction. Structure, services, levels and the site survey still need the engineers' and the municipality's review.*
