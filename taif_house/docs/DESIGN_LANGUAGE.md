# Taif / Palm House · design language

**Theme: modern classic with a Taif spirit.** Calm, warm, simple; the Taif references are in the stone, the arches, the colonnade and a little mashrabiya, not in ornament. Recorded 30 Oct 2026 (owner); every study and detail from here on follows it. The 3D character study (`3d/character.yaml`) is to be brought in line with this list when it is next touched.

*V9.0 (layout freeze, 6 Nov 2026):* the plan is frozen; the facades and the details are the next design stage and follow this list and the facade principle R-37 (the house stands inside a fully walled private farm: the facades are designed for light, views, climate and composition, not for privacy from outside). The 3D now shows the existing farm round the house (the old house, its covered parking, the walls and the gate, the palms) as plain approximate masses, not as design.

**9 Nov 2026 (owner): NO stone on the exterior.** No stone and no stone cladding anywhere outside, the malqaf tower included: every exterior finish is *render (plaster) and paint*. The character comes from the window proportions, the arches, the depth of the recesses and the projections that make shadows, all *built into the structure* in block and concrete, not applied later. Colours: a light sand render; an optional calm honey tone on the projections; a smooth or lightly textured exterior paint. Builder's rules: plaster mesh over every block / concrete junction before rendering; drips and falls on every projection from the start. **This supersedes every "honey stone" on the exterior below** (the plinth and the corners, the colonnade's columns and the arcade, the cornices and copings, the malqaf, the ladder's pilaster): read them as the same forms in render, the projections in the honey tone. **Floors too (owner, 9 Nov 2026): no stone and no marble on any floor, inside or outside: porcelain tiles everywhere**: inside a marble-look porcelain; outside (the courtyard, the colonnade, the riwaq, the paths, the guest path) an outdoor anti-slip porcelain (R11) in a sand / honey tone. Every "stone" or "marble" floor below reads as this porcelain. The facade study (windows, shaping, finishes) follows the plan freeze (open items 20, 21).

| Facade principle (owner, 3 Nov 2026) | The house stands inside a large, fully walled private farm (a 4 m north gate and a distant south gate; everything else walled and closed). Blank walls only where a room needs them (furniture, structure, services) or where the owner chooses privacy from the guests' path to the majlis (west side). To be applied at the facade design stage (no geometry change now): the mashrabiya stays a climate and composition element, not a privacy screen, except on the guests' west side. |
