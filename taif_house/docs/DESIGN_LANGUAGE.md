# Taif / Palm House · design language

**Theme: modern classic with a Taif spirit.** Calm, warm, simple; the Taif references are in the stone, the arches, the colonnade and a little mashrabiya, not in ornament. Recorded 30 Oct 2026 (owner); every study and detail from here on follows it. The 3D character study (`3d/character.yaml`) is to be brought in line with this list when it is next touched.

| Element | The language |
|---|---|
| Interior walls | Warm white (cream) plaster |
| Floors | Marble inside; matte honey-coloured stone in the courtyard (and under the colonnade) |
| Cornices | Very simple, thin |
| Exterior | Cream plaster with honey stone at the base (plinth) and the corners |
| Metal | Matte black; a few brass touches in the lighting |
| Timber | Medium-to-dark walnut (doors, soffits, built-ins) |
| Arches | Semicircular (Roman): the living's entrance arch and the TV arch |
| Windows and doors to outside | Black steel with divided lites |
| Colonnade | Round honey-stone columns (the three two-storey columns of the courtyard) |
| Stair railing | Simple black bar railing |
| Mashrabiya | Only in one or two places (for example the malqaf grille and one courtyard window), not as a theme |
| Lighting | Mostly hidden (coves, under shelves, in the arches); a few feature pieces (lanterns under the colonnade, one piece over the dining) |

How to read it: black steel and divided lites wherever glass meets the outside, including the courtyard enclosure (`studies/courtyard_enclosure.typ`); stone where the hand or the eye meets the ground (plinth, columns, courtyard floor, copings); walnut where the hand meets the house (doors, handrails, the TV cabinet); nothing gilded, nothing carved.
