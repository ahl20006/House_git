# Taif / Palm House · design language

**Theme: modern classic with a Taif spirit.** Calm, warm, simple; the Taif references are in the stone, the arches, the colonnade and a little mashrabiya, not in ornament. Recorded 30 Oct 2026 (owner); every study and detail from here on follows it. The 3D character study (`3d/character.yaml`) is to be brought in line with this list when it is next touched.

| Element | The language |
|---|---|
| Interior walls | Warm white (cream) plaster |
| Floors | Marble inside; matte honey-coloured stone in the courtyard (and under the colonnade) |
| Cornices | Very simple, thin |
| Exterior | Cream plaster with honey stone at the base (plinth) and the corners |
| Metal | Matte black; a few brass touches in the lighting |
| Timber | Medium-to-dark walnut (doors, soffits, built-ins) |
| Arches | Semicircular (Roman): the living's entrance arch and the TV arch; **V7.1:** the courtyard arcade with three arches under the projection (two slender stone columns between C21 and C22, courtyard bay only; the living bay keeps no arches) and arched heads on all the courtyard enclosure windows (six on the gallery edge over the arcade bays, two on the family living's edge, one large one over the living's courtyard glass): a two-tier classical arcade |
| Windows and doors to outside | Black steel with divided lites; the courtyard enclosure windows with semicircular heads (V7.1), the arched heads fixed, the operable lites below |
| Colonnade | Round honey-stone columns (the three two-storey columns of the courtyard) and, in the courtyard bay, two slender Ø0.25 intermediate columns of the arcade (V7.1) |
| Stair railing | Simple black bar railing; **V8.2:** the flipped stair's lower flight open to the riwaq behind a 0.90 m black-bar balustrade, the stair well's railing on the first floor the same |
| Mashrabiya | Only in a few places, not as a theme: the malqaf grille, the non-escape windows (office, suite lobby, women's majlis, dining), never on an escape window (V8.0 fire safety); **adopted (V8.2, owner):** the suite court's south side a mashrabiya screen (timber or black steel lattice, ≈ 40 % open) on the 1.10 m base up to the facade frame's soffit: the palms seen out, no view in |
| Lighting | Mostly hidden (coves, under shelves, in the arches); a few feature pieces (lanterns under the colonnade, one piece over the dining) |
| Roof and roofline (31 Oct 2026, V8.1) | Flat roof, no pitched or tiled roof anywhere. A strong honey-stone **main cornice** on the outer facade edge at roof level, all round. On the north block a raised roof screen (an "attic") set back ≈ 1.0 m behind the cornice, plastered cream with a stone coping, **1.40 m** above the roof finish (V8.1: lower and balanced; it still hides the plant) — a plain pentagon; the south wing (gym and office) keeps the cornice and a **low parapet 0.70 m**, the fall protection a black steel guardrail set back 1.0 m and not seen from the street, so the roofline steps ≈ 0.7 m; across the suite court's south side the cornice and the parapet run on as a facade frame. Above the attic only one vertical: the malqaf on its HRV base at the north-east. A simple thin stone cornice between the two floors. (`studies/roof.typ`) |
| Roof ladder (V8.1) | In the suite court, a honey-stone pilaster on the gym's east wall holds the maintenance ladder behind a flush walnut-finish panel: closed, the court reads a timber panel in a stone frame; the black steel stiles rise 1.10 m above the gym roof beside the court guard. |
| Courtyard (V8.0) | Matte honey-light stone floor with a honey-stone nosing on the seating step; built-in stone benches with cushions; the outdoor TV in a semicircular honey-stone surround (a visual frame, no cavity); one honey-stone box for the water basin (a laminar lip over stone, no spray) and the tree; no pergola. |

How to read it: black steel and divided lites wherever glass meets the outside, including the courtyard enclosure (`studies/courtyard_enclosure.typ`); stone where the hand or the eye meets the ground (plinth, columns, courtyard floor, copings); walnut where the hand meets the house (doors, handrails, the TV cabinet); nothing gilded, nothing carved.
| Coat cupboard and the store door (V8.2) | At the stair's start a full-height walnut coat cupboard (flush doors, no handles seen); the store under the half landing behind a low walnut door on the riwaq. |
| Site (V8.2, `studies/site_plan.typ`) | The guest path a 1.2 m band of honey stone along the driveway with black bollard lights; the garden's 1.2 m wall in honey stone with planting behind it; the hedge a clipped evergreen 1.8 m; the service yard screen plastered cream like the house with a stone base; the LPG cabinet matte black steel. The east stack (plumbing proposal) behind a removable panel of honey-stone slats on a black frame, on the service-yard face only. |
