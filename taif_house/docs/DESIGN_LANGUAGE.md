# Taif / Palm House · design language

**Theme: modern classic with a Taif spirit.** Calm, warm, simple; the Taif references are in the stone, the arches, the colonnade and a little mashrabiya, not in ornament. Recorded 30 Oct 2026 (owner); every study and detail from here on follows it. The 3D character study (`3d/character.yaml`) is to be brought in line with this list when it is next touched.

| Element | The language |
|---|---|
| Interior walls | Warm white (cream) plaster |
| Floors | Marble inside; matte honey-coloured stone in the courtyard (and under the colonnade) |
| Cornices | Very simple, thin |
| Exterior | Cream plaster with honey stone at the base (plinth) and the corners |
| Metal | Matte black; a few brass touches in the lighting |
| Timber | Medium-to-dark walnut (doors, soffits, built-ins) |
| Arches | Semicircular (Roman): the living's entrance arch and the TV arch; **V7.1:** the courtyard arcade with three arches under the projection (two slender stone columns between C21 and C22, courtyard bay only; the living bay keeps no arches) and arched heads on all the courtyard enclosure windows (six on the gallery edge over the arcade bays, two on the family living's edge, one large one over the living's courtyard glass): a two-tier classical arcade |
| Windows and doors to outside | Black steel with divided lites; the courtyard enclosure windows with semicircular heads (V7.1), the arched heads fixed, the operable lites below |
| Colonnade | Round honey-stone columns (the three two-storey columns of the courtyard) and, in the courtyard bay, two slender Ø0.25 intermediate columns of the arcade (V7.1) |
| Stair railing | Simple black bar railing |
| Mashrabiya | Only in a few places, not as a theme: the malqaf grille, the non-escape windows (office, suite lobby, women's majlis, dining), never on an escape window (V8.0 fire safety); **proposed** (V8.0, owner's choice): the suite court's south side as a mashrabiya screen in the honey-stone facade frame |
| Lighting | Mostly hidden (coves, under shelves, in the arches); a few feature pieces (lanterns under the colonnade, one piece over the dining) |
| Roof and roofline (31 Oct 2026, V8.0) | Flat roof, no pitched or tiled roof anywhere. A strong honey-stone **main cornice** on the outer facade edge at roof level, all round. On the north block a raised roof screen (an "attic") set back ≈ 1.0 m behind the cornice, plastered cream with a stone coping, 1.8 m high — a plain pentagon since V8.0 (no stair room); the south wing (gym and office) keeps only the cornice and a plain parapet, so the roofline steps; across the suite court's south side the cornice and the parapet run on as a facade frame. Above the attic only two verticals on the lane side: the malqaf on its HRV base at the north-east and the stair tower at the south-east. A simple thin stone cornice between the two floors. (`studies/roof.typ`) |
| Stair tower (V8.0) | An enclosed tower outside the east wall at the south-east: cream plaster, a honey-stone plinth and arch surrounds, two semicircular arched openings per storey on the east and south faces (unglazed, bird mesh), the north face solid, the main cornice line carried round it, a stone coping; black steel gates and rails. It must read as designed, not added on. |
| Courtyard (V8.0) | Matte honey-light stone floor with a honey-stone nosing on the seating step; built-in stone benches with cushions; the outdoor TV in a semicircular honey-stone surround (a visual frame, no cavity); one honey-stone box for the water basin (a laminar lip over stone, no spray) and the tree; no pergola. |

How to read it: black steel and divided lites wherever glass meets the outside, including the courtyard enclosure (`studies/courtyard_enclosure.typ`); stone where the hand or the eye meets the ground (plinth, columns, courtyard floor, copings); walnut where the hand meets the house (doors, handrails, the TV cabinet); nothing gilded, nothing carved.
