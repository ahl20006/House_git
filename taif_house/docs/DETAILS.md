## Structure

### West projection (1.35 m) on an edge beam and three columns
- **What:** a first-floor slab beyond the courtyard wall line x 4.25, along y 6.5–19.25, carrying the gallery and its railing.
- **Edge beam on x 5.6:** spans ≈ 6.5 m and 6.25 m. It is seen from the courtyard and the living, so express it as the colonnade's beam.
- **Three columns**, each in an existing wall or glass line, none free-standing in a room:
  - the women's majlis south wall (y 6.5);

- **Walls on walls:** the rooms' east wall (x 4.25) stands on the ground-floor courtyard edge line, and the first-floor outer walls on the ground-floor outer walls.

## Acoustics

### The gym and bedroom 3 (mandatory, V8.0)
- **The problem** is mostly structure-borne (footfall, the treadmill's motor); airborne noise second.
- **Wall** between the gym and bedroom 3 (y 19.25, x 0–4.25): a **double wall**, e.g. 200 mm dense block plus an independent stud lining with 50 mm mineral wool and two layers of 12.5 mm acoustic plasterboard; target **Rw ≈ 55 dB** (≈ 50 dB on site); sealed at the top; no sockets back to back, no duct through it; nothing fixed to it (the power rack is freestanding).
- **Floor:** a floating floor (resilient layer + screed, perimeter strip) or at least 20–40 mm dense rubber under the treadmill and the weights.
- **Treadmill:** anti-vibration pads; 0.5 m off the walls (the manufacturer-type zone: 2.0 m behind, 0.5 m each side); a cushioned deck and a quiet motor.
- **The office** is the suite bedroom's neighbour (quiet): a single 200 mm wall; the master bedroom has no door to it.

- **Sunken bath floors:** the suite bath (over the ground-floor kitchen) and the family bath (partly over the hospitality kitchen) carry all their pipes in the floor build-up; nothing hangs in a kitchen ceiling; no drain over the master bedroom.
- **Stacked wet rooms:** the bedroom 3 bath stands exactly over the ground-floor family WC; the family bath WC is on the west shaft wall.
- **Suite court (V8.0, near-flush doors):** waterproofing on the men's majlis roof slab in a depressed zone; the floor falls **south** at 1.5 % away from the three doors to a box gutter along the south side (two outlets, internal downpipes in the south wall) with an **overflow spout** through the south wall below the threshold level; **linear slot drains** in front of each door; the membrane turned up ≥ 150 mm behind the slot drains and under the door sills (a low-threshold detail: threshold ≤ 20 mm, the grating flush). The engineer details it.
- **Courtyard (V8.0):** the seating step's floor −0.15 falls to a drain at its south-east corner (a Ø100 gully with a silt bucket); an independent overflow gully at its north-east corner; the pipes 1 % east under the kitchen floor to the east-yard manhole; the water basin and the planter each with its own drain.
