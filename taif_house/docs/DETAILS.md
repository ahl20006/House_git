House-wide building rules and details, for the engineer, the carpenter and the contractor. The layout decisions and their reasons are in `PROJECT_V6.md`, and the open items in `OPEN_ITEMS.md`. *Concept notes, to be confirmed by the engineers.*

## Structure

### West projection (1.35 m) on an edge beam and three columns
- **What:** a first-floor slab beyond the courtyard wall line x 4.25, along y 6.5–19.25, carrying the gallery and its railing.
- **Edge beam on x 5.6:** spans ≈ 6.5 m and 6.25 m. It is seen from the courtyard and the living, so express it as the colonnade's beam.
- **Three columns**, each in an existing wall or glass line, none free-standing in a room:
  - the women's majlis south wall (y 6.5);
  - a pier in the courtyard / living glass (y 13.0);
  - the living's south wall (y 19.25).

  Each column needs its own foundation.
- **The structural engineer sizes** the slab, the beam depth, the columns and the pads.
- **Ground floor:** it changes only by the columns, their foundations, the beam line and a lower ceiling under the projection.
- **Walls on walls:** the rooms' east wall (x 4.25) stands on the ground-floor courtyard edge line, and the first-floor outer walls on the ground-floor outer walls.

### Gym, gym arm and office over the men's majlis
- **No wall below:** the men's majlis has no inner wall. The walls on its roof with no wall below are:
  - y 21.25 (gym / suite court);
  - x 4.25 (office / gym route and gym);
  - x 5.6 (gym route / suite court);
  - **x 11.586**, the treadmill arm's west wall (V6.2).
- **Arm walls on walls:** the arm's north wall (y 19.25) is on the ground-floor living / men's majlis wall line, and its east wall is the outer wall.
- **Loads:** the treadmill is a dynamic load (≈ 1.5–2 kN in use), on a floating floor or dense rubber with pads. Engineer: light wall or beam on x 11.586; the slab for the treadmill's vibration and the floating floor's weight.

### Walls on the women's majlis roof
- **The y 3.0 partition** (kids' room / family living) stands over the women's majlis, which has no wall below. Use a light partition on the slab, or a beam.

## Acoustics

### The treadmill arm and the master bedroom (mandatory, V6.2)
- **The problem** is mostly structure-borne: footfall and motor vibration go into the slab and the wall. Airborne noise comes second.
- **Wall** between the arm and the suite bedroom (y 19.25, x 11.586–13.311, and its junctions):
  - a **double wall**, e.g. the 200 mm dense block plus an independent stud lining with 50 mm mineral wool and two layers of 12.5 mm acoustic plasterboard, or two block leaves with a filled cavity;
  - target **≈ 55 dB airborne** (Rw), ≈ 50 dB between the rooms on site;
  - seal it at the top; no sockets back to back, no duct through it.
- **Floor:** a **floating floor** (resilient layer + screed, perimeter isolation strip, stopped short of the bedroom wall) or at least 20–40 mm dense rubber tiles over the whole arm.
- **Treadmill:**
  - **anti-vibration pads** under its feet;
  - **0.1 m off the shared wall**, never fixed to it;
  - prefer a cushioned deck and a quiet motor.

## Drainage and services
- **No east duct:** the kitchen extract leaves straight through the east wall; the vent stack and the suite bath drain run in one stack on the outside face of the east wall, down to the east-yard manhole. Place the hood south of y ≈ 7.5. MEP engineer to confirm.
- **Sunken bath floors:** the suite bath (over the ground-floor kitchen) and the family bath (partly over the hospitality kitchen) carry all their pipes in the floor build-up; nothing hangs in a kitchen ceiling; no drain over the master bedroom.
- **Stacked wet rooms:** the bedroom 3 bath stands exactly over the ground-floor family WC; the family bath WC is on the west shaft wall.
- **Suite court:** waterproofing on the men's majlis roof slab. Since V6.2 its floor **falls west** to its drain, away from the treadmill arm at its east end. A railing (not a parapet) on the gym roof edge.
- **Gas, if used:** a cylinder cabinet outside the east wall in the service yard.

## Joinery and doors
- **Built-in cupboards in niches** (linen cupboard, bedroom 2 wardrobe niche):
  - **preferred:** a joinery box with its own 18 mm sides, top and frame, scribed to the walls;
  - **10 cm block jamb:** only where a side is open or a heavy sliding door needs a solid fixing, and then draw the niche 20 cm wider.
- **10 cm partitions** (an idea for later): they give a room +10 cm of clear width. Check any beam on the line, and the sound (plaster both sides, or an insulated drywall).
- **Sliding doors** to the office, the gym, the suite WC closet, the suite court and the dressing wardrobes.
