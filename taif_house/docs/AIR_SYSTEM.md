# Taif / Palm House · V7.1 · air system study v3 (final concept: one tower, two separate air paths)

**Every size and performance figure below is assumed, to be designed by the MEP engineer.** v3.1 adds the owner's decisions on the open items (1 Nov 2026): the assist fan, bird mesh only, the HRV in a base under the tower, the condensers south of the tower, the make-up inlet's position. The drawings are `output/air_system.pdf` (from `studies/air_system.typ`): the tower plan at the head, the breeze path in plan and long section, the breeze estimate, the ventilation airflow, the intake / exhaust separations, the ceiling heights, the controls and the maintenance table.

**v1 and v2 are withdrawn** (tags `air-p1`, `air-p2`): the walk-in tunnel, the underground chamber with human entry, the water channel, the evaporative pads and the deep earth pipes with their cooling claims are gone. The courtyard fountain is a separate system (`studies/courtyard_layout.typ`).

## The owner's goals (1 Nov 2026, design review E-01)
One tower, the symbol of the house, with two completely separate air paths: (a) a gentle natural breeze for the courtyard seating in pleasant weather (not cooling); (b) the fresh-air intake of the house's ventilation unit, all year. Low maintenance. No water in the air system, no evaporative pads, no buried earth pipes, no human entry anywhere.

## A · The tower
- The tower in the tip of the cut corner: ≈ 0.83 m² clear (assumed), rising 2.5 m above the roof (head +7.30 … +9.80), the one element above the attic.
- Split by an airtight partition into the **breeze shaft ≈ 0.54 m²** (west) and the **service compartment ≈ 0.21 m²** (east). It holds both; if the engineer thickens the walls, the smallest enlargement is 0.25 m on the cut side (a proposal, not applied).
- **Head:** four-sided (Yazd badgir type), louvred openings +8.20 … +9.40 on all faces, honey-stone surrounds, timber louvres. An undivided four-sided head short-circuits; the simplest arrangement that works: a thin + divider in the head only, with a lightweight gravity back-draught flap at the foot of each quarter, so only the windward quarter feeds the single shaft; no dividers in the shaft, no sensor, no motor.
- **Louvres and screens:** a weather louvre and a **wide 20 mm bird mesh only** (owner: no insect mesh on the breeze path, for more breeze and less cleaning; the HRV intake keeps its pre-filter and fine filter) on hinged frames cleaned from the roof strip. Their pressure loss counts for little: the head's net open area is ten times the channel's throat; the losses sit in the channel and the grille. No fine filter on the breeze path.
- **Damper:** a motorised blade damper across the breeze shaft near the top (≈ +8.0), a standard spring-return HVAC actuator (closes on power loss, ≈ 5 W), on a wall switch by the seating; the actuator inside the tower, reached by hand from the roof hatch in the tower's south face.
- **ERV intake compartment:** the service compartment's top 1.5 m is an airtight box with its own north louvre (0.8 × 0.5, bird mesh) and a slide-in ISO Coarse 60 % pre-filter reached from the roof hatch; below it the two Ø250 risers; the Ø315 intake duct drops inside the compartment straight into the base (no external duct).
- **The base (owner): the tower rises from a base that houses the HRV, one mass.** Footprint on the tower's west line and the cut facade line, y 1.0–3.4 (≈ 5.5 m² gross, ≈ 3.6 m² inside), top +9.10 = the attic coping, so nothing but the tower stands above the attic; 1.65 m clear inside: a low service compartment, not a room. The unit sits on a plinth right behind a pair of 1.5 m doors on the west face (onto the roof terrace inside the attic): filters and the core are pulled out from the roof, nobody enters. Airtight from the tower's two paths; the exhaust louvre on the cut face. The malqaf becomes a two-stage mass at the north-east corner (base +9.10, tower +9.80), answering the stair room (+9.70) at the north-west.

## B · The courtyard breeze path
- **Route:** from the shaft's foot straight south on x 10.2 under the maid bath, the laundry walkway and the kitchen walkway, one 90° bend west under the sink counter into the plenum in the wall base and the grille under the outdoor TV: ≈ 10.5 m axis, one bend.
- **A shallow channel in the ground slab:** clear 0.90 × 0.20 (0.18 m²) from the slab top (−0.08, the covers flush with the floor) to the slab underside (−0.28), its own base slab below; through each wall crossed (y 1.0, 2.8, 7.0) a 0.9 × 0.2 slot in the wall foot above the ground beam's top: no beam, footing or floor cut or lowered. Under the maid bath a throat 0.5 × 0.20 between the shower and the WC (the WC moved to the cut-wall corner). The floor falls 0.5 % back to the tower's sump (−0.40, a Ø75 drain with a waterless dry trap to the east yard).
- **Access without entry:** 8 removable sealed covers (0.9 × 0.8, stone-faced stainless trays, gasketed) in the laundry and kitchen walkways, lifted while standing; the throat and the sump brushed with a rod from the first cover and the shaft's foot; the plenum from the courtyard with the grille removed.
- **Outlet:** the decorative grille under the TV, 1.0 × 0.35 at +0.05 … +0.40 (0.20 above the seating floor), a manually adjustable louvre directing the air along the floor to the benches, clear of the cushions and 1.6 m from the fountain's splash; lifts off for cleaning.
- **Assist fan (owner: yes):** a quiet EC extract-type fan box (two Ø250 EC axial fans at low speed, ≈ 0.9 × 0.3 × 0.3) in the plenum directly behind the grille, pulling the air down from the tower: ≈ 0.15–0.25 m³/s, ≤ 30 dB(A) at the benches, 40–60 W, on its own switch beside the seating (separate from the damper switch; runs only with the damper open). Service without any confined space: the grille lifts off and the box slides out into the courtyard.
- **The breeze, honestly** (concept estimate, assumed loss coefficients): weak wind (3 m/s at the head) ≈ 0.16 m³/s → ≈ 0.27 m/s felt at the benches, barely perceptible; moderate wind (6 m/s) ≈ 0.32 m³/s → ≈ 0.5 m/s, a gentle pleasant breeze; still air (stack only) nothing felt. The breeze is perceptible only with a moderate wind at the head; the assist fan gives it on any evening. Taif wind data to be checked.

## C · The house ventilation (the sealed house)
- **One unit in the tower's base on the roof** (`studies/roof.typ` Z2a, see A): ≈ 3.6 m² inside, 1.65 m clear, the unit's front at the base's west doors. Unit assumed: a 750 m³/h class counterflow unit ≈ 1.2 × 0.7 × 0.7 m, ≈ 110 kg, 90 W normal / ≈ 350 W boost.
- **HRV rather than ERV:** Taif is dry and high; moisture recovery gives little; a sensible-only core (or a swappable one) is the better fit; the MEP engineer decides.
- **Airflow** (ASHRAE 62.2-2022 §4.1.1, 0.15 L/s·m² + 3.5 L/s per bedroom + 1; ≈ 470 m² clear, 5 bedrooms): ≈ 92 L/s = 330 m³/h continuous; guests (24 in the majlis at the ASHRAE 62.1 assembly rate): boost ≈ 196 L/s = 705 m³/h; the unit covers it. To be confirmed against SBC 501 / 1101.
- **Intake and exhaust:** the intake from the tower compartment (north face at the head); the exhaust on the base's cut (north-east) face, low, blowing east. **The AC condensers stay south of the tower** (owner; zone Z2b from y 5.6): 2.2 m clear of the base's south wall, 5.15 m south of the intake in plan, the intake 1 m higher and facing north. Separations from the plan: intake ↔ exhaust ≈ 3.3 m (≥ 3), ↔ kitchen hood outlet 12.8 m (≥ 4.6), ↔ plumbing vent 8.7 m (≥ 3), ↔ LPG cabinet 12.7 m (≥ 4.6), ↔ generator point 15.3 m (≥ 9), ↔ nearest condenser 5.2 m (≥ 2), exhaust ↔ condensers 2.7 m; basis ASHRAE 62.1-2022 Table 5-1 (not verified against SBC 501).
- **Filters:** a coarse pre-filter in the tower compartment plus one fine filter at the unit's supply, provisionally ISO ePM1 ≥ 50 % (to confirm with the equipment); a pressure switch or the unit's timer as the service indicator.
- **Supply** to every bedroom, the living, the family living, the kids' room, the office and the men's majlis; **extract** from every bathroom, WC and the laundry; transfer air by 15–20 mm door undercuts or transfer grilles, so the system works with the bedroom doors closed.
- **The kitchen hood stays separate** (≈ 750 m³/h out through the east wall) with a filtered, motorised make-up air inlet (≈ 300 × 300), proposed discreetly on the kitchen's east wall at y 12.2–12.5 just under the first-floor stone band (+3.0 … +3.3, a louvre painted the wall colour; the fridge and tall storage stand below it inside), ducted in the ceiling to a diffuser 1.5 m from the hood, interlocked with the hood, so the heavy daily cooking never depressurises the house.
- **Ducts and ceilings:** two Ø250 risers in the tower's service compartment to both ceiling zones; flat ovals where the zone is thin. Finished ceilings: ≥ +3.07 in the ground-floor rooms (+3.17 under the 0.20 slabs), +2.92 in the kitchen (the hood duct), +2.90 only in the 0.3 m band under beam B5 at the dining's west edge; the first floor ≥ +6.75. No room gets too low.
- **Controls:** continuous quiet running with boost (kitchen, bathrooms, a "guests" button); the courtyard damper independent and spring-closed; a dust-event mode at the minimum balanced rate (no depressurisation; the unit is not assumed to recirculate — indoor air cleaning is separate portable equipment); on power failure the unit stops and the damper closes, the house stays sealed; automatic restart.

## E · Maintenance (initial intervals)
| Component | Task | From | Interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head louvres and bird mesh | brush / vacuum | the roof strip, hinged frames | 6 months, after storms |
| Back-draught flaps | check, clean | the roof hatch | 12 months |
| Damper and actuator | cycle, check the spring return | the roof hatch | 12 months |
| Pre-filter (tower) | replace | the roof hatch | 3 months |
| Unit filters | replace | the roof: the base's doors | 3–6 months (indicator) |
| Core | wash | the roof: the base's doors | 12 months |
| Exhaust louvre, make-up inlet | clean, test | the roof / the kitchen | 6 months |
| Channel covers and channel | vacuum, check gaskets | the laundry and kitchen floors | 6 months, after storms |
| Throat and sump | rod, check the dry trap | the first cover, the shaft's foot | 6 months |
| Plenum and grille | vacuum, set the louvre | the courtyard | monthly |
| Assist fan box | vacuum, check bearings and interlock | the courtyard, through the grille opening | 12 months |
| Room grilles | vacuum | the rooms | 6 months |

No routine task requires entering a shaft, pit or buried space.
