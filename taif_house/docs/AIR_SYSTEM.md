# Taif / Palm House · V10.0 · air system v4 · the malqaf at L5 (one tower at the courtyard's north-west corner, two separate air paths; the owner's decisions D1–D11 of 9 Nov 2026)

**V10.0 (9 Nov 2026, owner; `studies/malqaf_L5.typ` adopted, variant V-c + head F3; the fall-back F2 if the structural engineer refuses the beam additions).** The malqaf moves from the north-east cut corner (V8.0 – V9.3, superseded) to the **courtyard's north-west corner**: an *independent RC core* (walls 0.20) from its own pad to the head, inside the covered strip under the first-floor projection, against the women's majlis's wall (rebuilt in RC); nothing taken from the majlis or the family living; *no bedroom touches it* (D10). The drawings are `output/air_system.pdf` (from `studies/air_system.typ` v4: the foot chamber in plan, the section through the core, the head, the dampers, the separations, the breeze estimate, the ventilation airflow, the controls and the maintenance table) and the six-phase study `output/malqaf_L5.pdf`. **Every size and performance figure here is assumed, to be designed by the MEP engineer** (open item 9); the structure by the structural engineer (open item 22).

**v3 / v3.1 (V8.0 – V9.3) are superseded:** the tower in the cut tip, the HRV in a base or a plant room on the roof, the 10.6 m breeze channel in the ground slab under the maid bath, the laundry and the kitchen, and the grille under the outdoor TV are gone. **v1 and v2 are withdrawn** (tags `air-p1`, `air-p2`). The courtyard water is a separate system (`studies/courtyard_layout.typ`).

## The owner's goals and decisions
One tower, the symbol of the house (seen from the courtyard where the family lives, not from the gate: accepted), with two completely separate air paths: **(a) a large natural breeze for the courtyard seating** in pleasant weather (the goal, D1: ≈ 4 × the L1 system's flow), unfiltered by design (a bird mesh only); **(b) the fresh air of the house**, filtered, all year, *without an HRV* (D2): a filtered supply fan keeping the house at a slight positive pressure. No water in the air system (D9: a mobile evaporative cooler is an *option*: its space, a power point and a drain only; the water point capped); no human entry anywhere; all maintenance from a door at ground level. **Simple controls (owner, 9 Nov 2026):** two wall switches and one dust-sensor relay; every damper spring-return; no controller, no modes. **No stone:** render and paint; the honey tone on the band, the crown and the slot surrounds.

## C · Path (a): the courtyard breeze
- **Route:** the head's windward face → the flaps → D1 → the shaft (0.84 m²) → the dust trap at its foot → the chamber → the louvres and D2 → the seating. No channel, one turn.
- **The breeze, honestly** (`studies/air_system.typ` § 4, assumed loss coefficients referred to a 0.10 m² section: the L5 chain ≈ 0.12 against V9.3's 1.50): at the summer-evening mean wind (21 km/h at the mast) ≈ **1.8 m³/s**, ≈ 2.1 m/s at the louvres and ≈ 0.5–0.8 m/s at the seating 2 m away: *a clearly felt breeze*, ≈ 3.6–4.3 × the L1 system; a gentle 10 km/h wind ≈ 0.85 m³/s; a calm evening little (the stack ≈ 0.3 m³/s). About 1 800 pleasant, dust-free seating hours a year (Phase 0). The family closes the switch when it is too much.
- **The cooler (D9, option):** a 90–150 L portable evaporative cooler in the chamber moves ≈ 0.8–1.4 m³/s and cools the dry afternoon air from ≈ 35 to ≈ 20–22 °C (RH 12–20 %, ≈ 15–25 L/h of water); it cools *the people in the jet*. Provided now: the space, a power point, the floor drain; the water point capped; the malqaf is a *dry system*.

## D · Path (b): the house's fresh air (no HRV)
- **The inlet:** a downward-facing hood under the head's south overhang (+7.6), a bird mesh, **never closes** → a Ø315 duct down the chamber's south-east corner → a washable **ISO Coarse 60 % (G4) pre-filter** + an **ISO ePM1 ≥ 50 % (F7 class) fine filter** → a quiet **EC fan 750 m³/h at 250 Pa** (≈ 160 W) on anti-vibration mounts → a **0.9 m lined silencer** (≈ −15 dB; ≈ 38 dB(A) in the chamber, nothing heard in the courtyard through the 200 RC wall) → a sealed Ø250 riser in the shaft's west side → high grilles (+6.4) into the **family living** (the core's north face) and the **gallery** (south face), and at the GF (+2.7) into the **riwaq** (west face) and the **women's majlis** (north wall); the other rooms through the open plan and 15 mm door undercuts or transfer grilles. *No bedroom is ducted from the core.*
- **Airflow** (ASHRAE 62.2-2022 § 4.1.1: 0.15 L/s·m² + 3.5 L/s per bedroom + 1; ≈ 470 m² clear, 5 bedrooms): ≈ **330 m³/h normal**; the men's majlis full (24 guests, ASHRAE 62.1 assembly): ≈ **705 m³/h boost**; the fan covers it with the filters loaded. To be confirmed against SBC 501 / 1101.
- **Positive pressure:** the supply exceeds the continuous extracts (the bathrooms ≈ 150–250 m³/h, the dryer): ≈ +5–10 Pa; the house tight (D5); during a 150 µg/m³ dust event the house stays at roughly a quarter of the outdoor level. **The kitchen hood** (600–1 000 m³/h) exceeds the fan: **its interlocked, filtered, motorised make-up inlet (≈ 300 m³/h, opens with the hood) is mandatory** (owner) — without it the house would go negative and pull dust through every crack.
- **Winter:** the breeze closed, the fan at normal: ≈ 1.1 kW of heat at a 10 K difference (no heat recovery): small; boost when the house is full.
- **Filters:** a mechanical gauge on the cabinet; the fine filter changed at 200 Pa (≈ 6 months), the pre-filter washed monthly.

## E · Dampers and controls (simple)
- **D1** (the shaft's head) and **D2** (behind the outlet): insulated blades, low-leakage, **spring-return (closed on power loss)**, driven together. **No D3** (the gallery grille is a sealed provision). **D4:** a passive gravity louvre Ø160 from the chamber to the outlet, so the chamber never stays damp. The hood's make-up damper: its own, interlocked with the hood.
- **Switch 1 · MALQAF open / closed:** D1 + D2 open for the courtyard breeze whenever the family finds the evening pleasant; closed in winter, dust and when away. **Switch 2 · HOUSE FAN normal / boost:** the filtered path never stops; boost for guests, a full house, after a dust day (two speeds set once at commissioning).
- **The dust relay:** an outdoor PM2.5 sensor in a weather hood on the head's north face: above **50 µg/m³ for 10 min** D1 and D2 close by themselves and the switch's lamp shows "dust"; they reopen when the air has been below 25 µg/m³ for 30 min and the switch is on. A manual override on each actuator for the technician. *No controller, no modes.*
