# Taif / Palm House · V8.3 · master document

Concept design of a two-storey courtyard house in Taif. This document describes the whole house as of **V8.3** (git tag `v8.3`, 3 Nov 2026, a checkpoint): V8.0 (fire safety, the courtyard layout, the air system, the south wing v2), V8.1 (the roof ladder, the roofline, the office from the court, one site entry), V8.2 (the stair flip, bedroom 1 without a bath, the site and services plan, the plumbing study) and V8.3 (bedroom 2's en-suite and the family WC upstairs; the ground-floor powder room, no ground-floor shower; the level courtyard with movable seating, the fountain and tree in front of the kitchen window, the TV moved south; the outdoor kitchen by the service door; the plumbing decisions; the facade principle R-37; bedroom 1's hotel en-suite studied, not applied; §4.47–4.53). The open items are listed in `OPEN_ITEMS.md`, and the next steps are in `NEXT_SESSION.md`. The figures below come from the project files: `data/*.csv`, the design files and the studies. They match the presentation `output/Taif_House_V8.3_presentation.pdf` (12 sheets; sheet 01 the site and services plan, sheet 06 the south wing and the suite, sheet 09 the schedules, sheets 11–12 the roof and the elevations). The plumbing study (`studies/plumbing.typ`) and the site plan (`studies/site_plan.typ`) are studies: their positions and sizes are proposals until the survey and the engineers confirm them.

*Concept design, not for construction. Structure, services, levels and the site survey still need the engineers' and the municipality's review.*

## 4. Design decisions and their reasons

1. **Courtyard house.** A small, shaded courtyard at the centre, enclosed on four sides and open to the sky; the rooms the family uses most open onto it; quiet outer walls with small openings.
2. **One living room, two heights.** The courtyard half double height (a chimney with high windows), the east half (the dining) normal height with the first floor above.
3. **West service band.** The family entrance, the lift, the stair (with the coat cupboard in its start strip, V8.2), the utility room with the shaft and the west wash block in one band: services rise in one place, one stiff core.
4. **West wash block and the two-door serving passage.** One strip of wet rooms on one drain run; the guest side has no door onto the riwaq; the only link is a passage with two doors never open together, both locked from the family side.
5. **Hospitality kitchen.** A light kitchen beside the serving passage; the east kitchen is the heavy one.
6. **Guest foyer and majaz.** The double door at one end of the foyer and the majlis opening at the other (partial in the short foyer: open item 13).
7. **Trough basins.** One trough with three taps for the guests.
8. **Men's majlis.** Full width on the south, entered only from the foyer; high south windows; the main seating at the east end.
9. **East service block and maid wing.** The kitchen on the courtyard lines, isolated; the laundry along the women's majlis wall; the maid room and bath in the sloped corner, reached only through the kitchen and the laundry. The maid room meets the municipal minimum since V7.0 (§4.26).
10. **Air tower** (V8.0: §4.33). The tower fills the tip of the cut corner. The V1 tunnel, the V2 dry duct and their water / pad / earth-pipe ideas are withdrawn; see §4.33.
11. **Lift at construction, two stops (V6.3).** Ground and first floor; the shaft 2.25 × 2.2 a placeholder; the lift does not serve the roof (the roof by the maintenance ladder, V8.1).
12. **Utility room and shaft.** The west shaft inside a utility room with the panel, the manifold and the water heater.
13. **Plumbing strategy** (V8.2: the plumbing study rounds 1–2, `studies/plumbing.typ`; V8.3 decisions, §4.49: the east stack external behind removable cladding, automatic trap primers on the rarely used drains, rain and sewage fully separate with the condensate by tundishes; the suite bath's approach still open). Two clusters, no third: west round the shaft (one buried run under the wash block, now falling *north* to the driveway's chamber), east along the east wall (kitchen, laundry, maid bath buried east to the yard's foul chamber; the suite bath to one stack outside the east wall behind removable cladding). Both to a collector chamber inside the gate and under the wall to the septic near C1. Rain fully separate: the courtyard's drain and overflow and the suite court's gutter to their own chambers and soakaways. Every size assumed, to be designed by the plumbing engineer.
14. **First-floor zoning.** The family part on the north and west around the courtyard; the south wing (V8.0: the gym west, the suite court, the office east) and the master suite to the south and east.
15. **West projection on columns** (V5.0; restored in V6.1; 1.55 m since V7.0). The gallery on a 1.55 m projection over the courtyard and the void, carried by an edge beam on x 5.80 on three columns in existing wall or glass lines; the first-floor west wall on the ground-floor west wall, 0.785 m from the M line. *History:* the split projection of V5.1 was reverted in V6.1 (branch `v5-split-projection`, tag `v6.0`).
16. **Kids' room.** Study and gaming on the north side; a fourth bedroom later.
17. **Family living on the courtyard.** A view, not a path.
18. **Bedrooms.** Bedroom 1 one room without a bath (decided V8.2, option A, with night lights on its route); bedroom 2 with a wardrobe niche; bedroom 3 with its own bath; one larger family bath by the shaft with a separate WC compartment (F-b, V8.2).
19. **Master suite.** A lobby to the bath, the dressing and the bedroom (a bent entry); the bedroom on the suite court; V8.0: no door to the office, an entry zone at its court door (§4.35).
20. **Owner's office.** On the east beside the suite (V8.0); for the owner only, reached from the suite court; visitors are met in the men's majlis (V8.1, §4.37).
21. **Gym.** V8.0: on the west band (§4.35). *History:* the L-shaped gym of V6.2 (the treadmill alone in an arm at the east end against the suite bedroom) is replaced.
22. **Suite court and the clerestory light.** V8.0: the court runs from the gym to the office and to the south facade (§4.35); the double-height living's two clerestory windows (+5.00 … +6.00) get the winter noon sun on their whole height (V7.1: 81 %), the obstruction seen from them falls from 40° to 31°. Their privacy: the sill is 1.40 m above the court floor; they stay openable, as drawn (decided V8.2; the court is no escape route, so no fire-resisting glazing is needed).
